# 📊 Insurance Data Analysis | Excel & Tableau

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing insurance business data using Microsoft Excel and Tableau. The main objective is to understand business performance by exploring information related to budgets, meetings, invoices, business opportunities, revenue, and policy renewals.

I used Excel data as the source and Tableau to explore the data and present business insights through interactive visualizations.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze insurance business performance across different branches.
- Understand employee budgets and business targets.
- Track meetings and invoice activities.
- Analyze new business opportunities and their stages.
- Monitor policy renewals and cross-selling activities.
- Identify top business opportunities.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

- **Microsoft Excel** – Data handling and spreadsheet analysis.
- **Tableau** – Data visualization and interactive dashboards.

## 📂 Project Files

| File Name | Description |
|---|---|
| `dashboard insurance.xlsx` | Excel workbook containing insurance business data and analysis sheets. |
| `Insurance Tableau.twbx` | Packaged Tableau workbook containing visualizations and dashboards. |

## 📈 Dashboard Components

The Tableau workbook includes the following visualizations:

1. **Funnel Chart** – Displays the business opportunity funnel.
2. **New Business** – Provides an overview of new business activities.
3. **New Business Bar Chart** – Visualizes new business data.
4. **No. of Executives** – Shows executive-related information.
5. **No. of Invoices** – Displays invoice-related information.
6. **Renewal** – Covers policy renewal activities.
7. **Top 10 Opportunities** – Highlights the top business opportunities.
8. **Yearly Meeting** – Displays yearly meeting information.
9. **Cross Sell** – Covers cross-selling activities.
10. **Cross Sell Bar Chart** – Visualizes cross-selling data.
11. **Renewal Bar Chart** – Visualizes renewal-related data.

## 🔍 Key Areas of Analysis

- Branch-wise business budgets.
- Employee budget allocation.
- Meeting and invoice trends.
- Revenue and brokerage information.
- Opportunity stages and potential business.
- Policy renewal and cross-selling activities.
